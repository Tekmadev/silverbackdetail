# MOBILE VERSUS IN-SHOP

Mobile is a way Silverback delivers its services, not a separate package or a separate price list. Any detail that finishes in under a day can be done at a home or workplace: exterior detail, interior detail, and headlight restoration.

The mobile unit is self-contained and carries its own water and power. All that is needed is a parking spot with a bit of room to work around the vehicle.

Paint correction and ceramic coating are multi-day jobs that need a controlled environment for polishing and curing, so they are in-shop only at 981 Main Street West.

# HOW THE WORK IS DONE

The four-step process on every vehicle:

1. **Inspect.** Assess paint, interior, and problem areas under proper lighting. Paint depth is recorded where correction is involved.
2. **Prep.** Decontamination, careful masking, and surface preparation. The unglamorous work that makes the finish last.
3. **Refine.** Multi-stage polishing, deep cleaning, and conditioning. Where the transformation happens.
4. **Protect.** Sealants and ceramic coatings applied and cured, then a final inspection before the keys come back.

Detailers work to manufacturer specifications with hands-on training in machine polishing and professional ceramic coating application.

# COMMON QUESTIONS

**How much does ceramic coating cost?**
Ceramic coating starts at $1,200 CAD and includes full paint correction prep. Final price depends on vehicle size, paint condition, and whether the customer picks the 2, 5, or 9 year package.

**How does paint correction work?**
Paint correction is multi-stage machine polishing that removes swirl marks, light scratches, oxidation, and water spots. Paint depth is measured first, then the clear coat is refined in stages to restore a true mirror gloss. Starts at $800 CAD, typically 1 to 2 days.

**How long does a ceramic coating last?**
Rated for 2, 5, or 9 years depending on the package. Longevity also depends on maintenance, and an aftercare kit plus guidance comes with every coating.

**Ceramic coating versus wax, what is the difference?**
Wax or sealant gives a warm gloss and lasts roughly 3 to 6 months, and it is what comes with an exterior detail. Ceramic coating bonds to the paint and is rated in years, with hydrophobic, UV, and chemical resistance. Ceramic requires paint correction prep first, which is why it costs more.

**Do you offer mobile detailing?**
Yes. Exterior detail, interior detail, and headlight restoration can be done at a home or workplace anywhere in Hamilton, Burlington, Ancaster, Stoney Creek, Dundas, or Waterdown. The unit carries its own water and power. Paint correction and ceramic coating stay in-shop.

**Is my deposit refundable?**
Yes, fully refundable up to 48 hours before the scheduled service, and credited toward the final invoice on the day of service.

**What areas do you serve?**
Hamilton, Burlington, Ancaster, Stoney Creek, Dundas, and Waterdown. In-shop at the Hamilton studio, mobile across the full area.

**How do I prepare my car?**
Remove personal belongings, and child seats if those areas should be cleaned. For mobile appointments, provide a parking spot with a little room to work around the vehicle. Silverback handles everything else.

**Do you guarantee your work?**
Yes. Every service is backed by a satisfaction guarantee, and coatings come with clear written longevity expectations.

**Can you get scratches out?**
Light scratches, swirls, oxidation, and water spots come out with paint correction. Scratches deep enough to catch a fingernail have gone through the clear coat and cannot be safely polished out. That gets assessed in person.

**Do I need to be there during the service?**
No. Drop the keys off for in-shop work. For mobile, the vehicle just needs to be accessible.

**How often should I get my car detailed?**
Most drivers do a full detail every 3 to 6 months. More often for anyone with kids, pets, or heavy daily use.

**Do you take walk-ins?**
Bookings need 48 hours of lead time and slots fill up, so call ahead rather than driving over.

**Do you do window tinting, paint protection film, dent removal, bodywork, or mechanical repair?**
No. None of those are offered. Silverback is glad to detail the vehicle after that work is done elsewhere.
